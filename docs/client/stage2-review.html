What the demo shows

The attached video records the real player and the real broadcast on the test server, including the audio. Each test mix has a voice saying "minute N" once a minute, so you can hear where in the mix the station is.

Key point: this is radio, not a playlist

When a listener presses Play, they join the station at its current point. In the demo, playback is paused at 2:33 into a mix and resumed 80 seconds later at 3:54, because the station kept broadcasting while nobody was listening. Stations run 24/7 whether or not anyone is tuned in.

What's needed from you to go live

  1. Approve the approach and hosting. AzuraCast plus our station list service, on an OVH UK server at about £17 a month plus VAT.
  2. Domain names. For example radio.yourdomain.com.
  3. OnTop FM. Stream address and written permission from the owner.
  4. Content. Real mixes, adverts and station logos. Confirm adverts every ~hour (after every two ~30-minute parts) is the right frequency.
  5. Licensing. PPL and PRS for Music before public launch. The service description is ready to send.
  6. iPhone volume. Accept that on iPhone, volume is set with the phone's buttons (an Apple rule for all web players).
  7. Real-device testing. A short session on an iPhone and an Android phone using the checklist, once the live server is up.

Worth knowing

After a station is restarted, AzuraCast starts the next mix from its beginning rather than resuming the interrupted one. Normal 24/7 running is unaffected.

Licensing bodies usually want reports of individual tracks played. Since stations play whole DJ mixes, tracklists from the DJs will be needed for that reporting.
